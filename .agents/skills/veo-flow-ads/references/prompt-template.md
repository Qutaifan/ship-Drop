# Veo 3.1 Prompt Template — 9:16 Vertical Ad

Order matters. Subject first, then setting, motion, camera, audio. ≤80 words. Always end with the EU AI Act safety footer.

```
[Subject, 1 sentence]. [Setting, 1 sentence]. [Motion, 1 sentence describing what happens in 8s]. [Camera, 1 phrase: lens + movement]. [Audio, 1 sentence: sfx + voiceover if any].

[Safety footer]: Photorealistic 9:16 vertical video for a paid social ad. EU AI Act disclosure overlay will be added in post.
```

## Worked example — electric pepper grinder

```
Close-up of a hand twisting an electric pepper grinder over a sizzling ribeye steak on a wooden board. Restaurant table, warm tungsten light, marble counter. Hand presses button; grinder body glows amber; pepper falls in slow motion; steam rises. Macro lens, slow dolly-in. Audio: grinder motor hum, peppercorn crack on steak, soft sizzle.

Photorealistic 9:16 vertical video for a paid social ad. EU AI Act disclosure overlay will be added in post.
```

## Worked example — folding laundry basket

```
Sweaty post-gym clothes piled on a tile floor next to a clunky plastic laundry basket. Small apartment, morning light. Hand reaches in, basket collapses to 5cm flat in one motion; person slides it into a drawer. Eye-level, handheld follow. Audio: plastic snap, drawer slide, breath of relief.

Photorealistic 9:16 vertical video for a paid social ad. EU AI Act disclosure overlay will be added in post.
```

## Anti-patterns

- ❌ *Ad copy in the prompt*: "Buy now for €49.90 with free shipping!" — Veo doesn't render text reliably; the brief belongs in PROTOCOL-02 landing-page copy, not in the video prompt.
- ❌ *Brand logos*: Veo will not render a real brand mark; product reference image is the right path.
- ❌ *Stacked adjectives*: "stunning, cinematic, breathtaking, gorgeous, premium" — pick one visual register.
- ❌ *More than 80 words*: coherence drops; subject drift increases.
- ❌ *No audio line*: Veo will invent one anyway; specify to avoid clipping / wrong-tone sfx.
