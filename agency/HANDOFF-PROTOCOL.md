# Cross-Department Handoff Protocol

## Overview
This document defines the formal handoff protocols between departments to ensure smooth workflow and knowledge transfer throughout the product lifecycle.

## Handoff Sequence

### 1. Market Intelligence → Product Validation
**Trigger**: Completion of market research and opportunity identification
**Deliverables**:
- Candidate list with market analysis
- Competitor intelligence reports
- Demand validation metrics
- Risk assessment

**Quality Gates**:
- Minimum 5 candidates per review cycle
- 5-10 competitors identified per product
- Demand confidence ≥60%
- Market saturation below 15 advertisers

**Communication Protocol**:
- Structured candidate brief template
- Shared market intelligence database
- Regular sync meetings for new findings

### 2. Product Validation → Creative Production
**Trigger**: Successful validation through all PROTOCOL-01 gates
**Deliverables**:
- Validated product specifications
- True Margin Matrix calculations
- Creative brief requirements
- Target metrics and constraints

**Quality Gates**:
- CAC gate passed (2x median CPA)
- True Margin Matrix validated
- Retail band within EUR 62-93
- All 6-Criteria met

**Communication Protocol**:
- Validated product package transfer
- Margin and pricing constraints documentation
- Creative direction alignment

### 3. Creative Production → Campaign Operations
**Trigger**: Completion of 3+1 creative briefs and assets
**Deliverables**:
- 3 video hooks (problem, transformation, aspirational)
- Landing page framework
- Remotion programmatic video scaffold
- Creative asset package

**Quality Gates**:
- All 3 hooks completed
- Landing page structure approved
- Remotion scaffold validated
- Creative assets pass quality review

**Communication Protocol**:
- Asset handoff checklist
- Creative specifications documentation
- Platform-specific formatting requirements

### 4. Campaign Operations → Supply Chain
**Trigger**: Campaign launch and first orders received
**Deliverables**:
- Order volume predictions
- Fulfilment requirements
- Inventory needs assessment
- Lead time coordination

**Quality Gates**:
- Order volume forecast accuracy
- Fulfilment capacity confirmed
- Inventory levels adequate
- EU warehouse readiness

**Communication Protocol**:
- Demand forecast sharing
- Inventory level alerts
- Lead time coordination
- Customer satisfaction feedback

### 5. All Departments → Financial Analytics
**Trigger**: Ongoing campaign operations
**Deliverables**:
- Real-time P&L tracking
- Campaign ROI analysis
- Budget utilization reports
- Margin performance data

**Quality Gates**:
- Accurate margin calculations
- ROAS tracking at ≥3.0x
- Budget adherence
- Compliance with financial protocols

**Communication Protocol**:
- Daily financial reports
- Weekly optimization reviews
- Monthly comprehensive analysis
- Quarterly strategic reviews

### 6. All Departments → Learning & Optimization
**Trigger**: Campaign closure (Kill/Scale/Iterate)
**Deliverables**:
- Campaign outcomes
- Prediction vs. actual analysis
- Root cause attribution
- Heuristic updates

**Quality Gates**:
- All campaigns produce written learning
- Prediction scoring completed
- Heuristic status updated
- No hypothesis fishing

**Communication Protocol**:
- Structured learning templates
- HEURISTICS.md updates
- Calibration log maintenance
- Cross-department knowledge sharing

1. **Shared Memory**: Cross-department context
2. **Session Search**: Historical knowledge retrieval
3. **File System**: Structured document sharing

### Conflict Resolution
1. **Priority Rules**: Risk management overrides opportunity
2. **Escalation Paths**: Clear paths for human oversight
3. **Decision Logs**: All major decisions documented
4. **Feedback Loops**: Continuous improvement mechanisms
