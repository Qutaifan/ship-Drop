# Supply Chain & Logistics Department - Hermes-Ecom Drop Shipping Agency

## Department Mandate
**Primary Function**: Optimize supplier network, inventory management, and fulfilment operations to support anti-de-minimis strategy and sustainable growth.

### Key Objectives:
- Establish EU warehouse network for anti-de-minimis compliance
- Optimize supplier relationships with CJdropshipping and other partners
- Implement real-time inventory tracking and management
- Minimize duty and tax exposure through strategic fulfilment
- Ensure reliable lead times and customer satisfaction
- Coordinate with campaign demand predictions

### Supply Chain Strategy (Hermes-Ecom):

#### Anti-De-Minimis Optimization:
1. **EU Warehouse Network**: Direct fulfilment from EU warehouses
2. **Duty Elimination**: €3 duty per customs item → €0 duty from EU fulfillment
3. **VAT Optimization**: IOSS/OSS registration for EU-wide compliance
4. **Cost Reduction**: Import VAT paid once on bulk, not per order

#### Supplier Management:
1. **Primary Partner**: CJdropshipping (free, per-fulfilment pricing)
2. **Secondary Partners**: eBay, Temu (as needed for specific products)
3. **Quality Standards**: Verified supplier performance and reliability
4. **Risk Mitigation**: Multiple suppliers for critical categories

## Department Protocols (Hermes-Ecom):

### Supplier Selection Criteria:
1. **Cost Structure**: Free membership, per-fulfilment pricing only
2. **Geographic Distribution**: EU warehouse presence for anti-de-minimis
3. **Performance Track Record**: Proven reliability and quality
4. **Flexibility**: Ability to scale with campaign demands
5. **Compliance**: Proper tax and customs documentation

### Inventory Management Protocol:
1. **Real-time Visibility**: Continuous inventory monitoring
2. **Predictive Replenishment**: Demand-based restocking
3. **Safety Stock**: Buffer levels for campaign spikes
4. **Vendor Managed Inventory**: Automatic reordering

### EU Fulfilment Strategy:
1. **Bulk Import**: One-time duty payment on EU warehouse stock
2. **Per-Order Fulfillment**: Zero additional duty per order
3. **VAT Compliance**: IOSS/OSS registration and reporting
4. **Cross-Border**: EU-wide shipping with local tax compliance

#### Fulfilment Risks:
