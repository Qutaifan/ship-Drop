# Campaign Operations Department - Hermes-Ecom Drop Shipping Agency

## Department Mandate
**Primary Function**: Launch, monitor, optimize, and scale validated campaigns while maintaining strict risk-adjusted return discipline.

### Key Objectives:
- Execute successful campaigns with ROAS ≥3.0x
- Monitor performance and optimize based on real data
- Implement scale/kill/iterate decisions
- Maintain aggressive risk reduction during drawdowns
- Protect capital through disciplined execution
- Feed performance data into learning loop

### Campaign Operations Workflow (Hermes-Ecom):

#### Launch Phase:
1. **Budget Allocation**: Protected capital, no over-leverage
2. **Platform Setup**: TikTok, Meta, Google Shopping integration
3. **Creative Integration**: Deploy creative production assets
4. **Tracking Setup**: Conversion pixels, attribution, performance metrics

#### Monitoring Phase:
1. **Real-time Tracking**: ROAS, CPA, CTR, CVR monitoring
2. **Performance Analysis**: Daily optimization recommendations
3. **Risk Management**: Aggressive reduction during drawdowns
4. **Budget Optimization**: Shift spend to high-performing channels

#### Optimization Phase:
1. **A/B Testing**: Systematic creative and messaging optimization
2. **Price Optimization**: Dynamic pricing based on demand signals
3. **Audience Targeting**: Refine lookalike audiences
4. **Creative Refresh**: Periodic creative rotation for freshness

## Department Protocols (Execution Discipline):

### Campaign Launch Protocol:
1. **Capital Protection**: No campaign exceeds 5% of available capital
2. **Risk-Adjusted Returns**: ROAS ≥3.0x requirement
3. **Performance Baselines**: Above industry averages before scaling
4. **Exit Strategy**: Predefined kill criteria

### Daily Operations:
1. **Performance Monitoring**: ROAS, CPA, CTR, CVR tracking
2. **Risk Assessment**: Drawdown detection and response
3. **Budget Reallocation**: Shift to high-performing campaigns
4. **Creative Refresh**: Optimize based on performance data

### Optimization Framework:
1. **A/B Testing**: Systematic testing of creative, messaging, targeting
2. **Price Optimization**: Dynamic pricing based on demand and competition
3. **Audience Refinement**: Lookalike audience expansion/contraction
4. **Channel Diversification**: Balance between paid and organic

### Risk Management (Hermes-Ecom):

#### Capital Protection:
- **Position Sizing**: Maximum 5% per campaign
- **Diversification**: Multiple campaigns across product categories
- **Stop Losses**: Aggressive reduction during 20%+ drawdowns
- **Liquidity Protection**: Immediate withdrawal capability

#### Execution Discipline:
- **No Emotional Trading**: Data-driven decisions only
- **No Revenge Trading**: Strict adherence to stop losses
- **No Overtrading**: Limited to high-confidence opportunities
- **No Oversizing**: Disciplined position sizing

#### Market Risks:
- **Volatility Spikes**: Immediate risk reduction
- **Liquidity Collapse**: Cash preservation mode
- **Uncertainty Expansion**: Conservative positioning
- **Correlation Instability**: Diversified exposure
