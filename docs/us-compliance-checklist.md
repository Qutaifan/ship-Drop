# US Compliance and Logistics Checklist

**Project:** dropship  
**Market:** US pilot  
**Status:** required before any product test

---

## Product Eligibility

- [ ] Product is not counterfeit, trademark-infringing, or brand-confusing.
- [ ] Product is not a restricted medical, ingestible, supplement, weapon, tobacco, vape, adult, surveillance, or regulated safety product.
- [ ] Product does not require FDA, FCC, CPSIA, Prop 65, UL/ETL, or other certification unless documentation is collected and verified.
- [ ] Product claims are limited to observable features and do not make medical, therapeutic, guaranteed-income, or deceptive performance claims.
- [ ] Product photos and creative do not imply false scarcity, fake endorsements, or unverified results.

---

## US Fulfillment Fit

- [ ] Supplier can ship to the US within the target delivery window.
- [ ] Preferred: supplier has US warehouse or US-friendly fulfillment route.
- [ ] Carrier options documented.
- [ ] Tracking availability documented.
- [ ] Lost/damaged shipment policy documented.
- [ ] Handling time documented separately from transit time.
- [ ] No hidden oversized, hazmat, battery, liquid, fragile, or temperature-sensitive shipping constraints.

---

## Returns and Support

- [ ] US return pathway documented.
- [ ] Return address or returnless-refund policy documented.
- [ ] Expected return reasons listed.
- [ ] Warranty/support burden estimated.
- [ ] Customer support script required if product has setup steps.
- [ ] Refund and replacement conditions documented before ad testing.

---

## Sales Tax and Checkout

- [ ] State sales-tax nexus status reviewed before live launch.
- [ ] Checkout tax settings documented before production storefront publish.
- [ ] Payment processor fee included in unit economics.
- [ ] Refund handling and tax reversal behavior documented.
- [ ] No dynamic personalized pricing based on protected traits, demographics, or behavioral profiling.

---

## Advertising Compliance

- [ ] No fake reviews or unverifiable testimonials.
- [ ] No deceptive countdowns or false stock scarcity.
- [ ] No before/after claims unless substantiated.
- [ ] AI-generated imagery disclosed where legally required or platform-required.
- [ ] Landing page offer matches ad claim, price, shipping window, and refund policy.

---

## Approval

A product may not enter paid testing unless this checklist is attached to the candidate file and signed off in the relevant phase approval artifact.
