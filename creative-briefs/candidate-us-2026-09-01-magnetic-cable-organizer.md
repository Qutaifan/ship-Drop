## The "3+1" Brief (PROTOCOL-02 / AGENTS.md §5)

### Ad Hook 1 — Problem-Oriented
- Hook (first 3s pain point): Charger cable drops behind the desk again right before a meeting.
- Script/storyboard beats:
  1. Close-up: hand reaches, cable disappears behind desk edge.
  2. Frustrated scramble under desk.
  3. Magnetic organizer snaps onto desk leg.
  4. Six cords lock into place in sequence.
  5. End frame: "Your desk stops fighting back."

### Ad Hook 2 — Transformation
- Before/after visual concept: chaotic desk cut to clean desk with perfectly separated cords.
- Script/storyboard beats:
  1. Split-screen mess vs clean.
  2. Fast match cuts: phone cable, keyboard cable, headset cable each clicking in.
  3. Top-down beauty shot of a minimalist desk.
  4. End frame: "6 cables. 1 clean system."

- Environment/setting: premium work-from-home desk with soft lamp light, laptop, mechanical keyboard, coffee.
- Script/storyboard beats:
  1. Slow pan across curated desk setup.
  2. Fingers place cables into matching colored clips.
  3. Calm productivity beat with phone dock and notebook.
  4. End frame: "Clean setup. Clear head."
